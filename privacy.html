1. Introduction and Who We Are

Welcome to Hazel (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how Intervada Solutions LLC, a limited liability company registered in the State of Wyoming, United States ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, stores, uses, protects, and handles your personal data when you use our mobile application, our website located at https://thehazel.app (the "Website"), and all related features, content, and services (collectively, the "Services").

  • Data Controller / Legal Entity: Intervada Solutions LLC
  • Registered Office Address: 30 N Gould St, STE R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States
  • Support & Privacy Inquiries: support@thehazel.app

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with global privacy standards, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("EU GDPR"), the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection ("FADP"), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), the Washington My Health My Data Act ("MHMDA"), Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 603A, Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act ("PIPEDA"), the Australian Privacy Principles ("APPs") under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020, and South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act ("POPIA").

Our Core Privacy Promise:
Your body, your family, and your data belong to you. We do not sell, rent, trade, or give away your personal information or health data to third parties. We never use your fertility, cycle, pregnancy, or infant care logs for third-party advertising, behavioral profiling, or data brokerage. Your information is processed strictly to provide, synchronize, secure, and improve the Services you request.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimization and collect only the information necessary to deliver the core functionality of the App across its Trying to Conceive (TTC), Pregnancy, and Infant Care chapters.

2.1. Personal Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you create an account using email/password, Sign in with Apple (including Apple’s Hide My Email relay if selected), or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, encrypted authentication credentials or OAuth identity token, optional preferred name/nickname, caregiver role title (e.g., Mother, Father, Partner), illustrated avatar selection, visual theme preference, and time zone.
  • Family Workspace Sharing Information (Up to 8 Members): If you create a Family Workspace or join an existing workspace using a 6-character Family Invite Code, we associate your authenticated User UUID with that shared workspace (supporting up to 1 Primary Owner + 7 invited family caregivers) so authorized members can view and log shared family routines.
  • Customer Support Communications: When you contact us via Contact Support or at support@thehazel.app, we collect your email address, selected topic, and message contents solely to assist you.

2.2. Sensitive Health, Maternal, and Infant Care Data You Voluntarily Log

Depending on which chapters and features of the App you choose to use, you may voluntarily enter health-related and developmental data ("Special Category Data" under the EU/UK GDPR and "Consumer Health Data" / "Sensitive Personal Information" under applicable U.S., Canadian, Australian, New Zealand, and Swiss laws):

  1. Trying to Conceive (TTC) & Cycle Tracking Data: Menstrual period start dates and durations, menstrual flow intensity, basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus observations, luteinizing hormone (LH) / ovulation test results, pregnancy test results, optional clinical context tags you select (such as PCOS, endometriosis, thyroid conditions, or irregular cycles), supplements/medications taken, physical symptoms, mood, and daily hydration intake.
  2. Pregnancy Data: Estimated due date (EDD), last menstrual period (LMP) or Day 3 / Day 5 IVF embryo transfer date, singleton/multiples indicator, maternal weight logs, fetal movement/kick counts, physical activity, rest and sleep logs, appetite, pregnancy symptoms, mood, hydration, and prenatal vitamins/medications taken.
  3. Infant Sleep & Daily Care Data: Your child’s name or nickname, birth date, optional premature birth original due date (used solely to compute Adjusted Age wake windows), wake times, nap start and end times, bedtimes, night wakings, breastfeeding sessions (duration and side), bottle feeding volumes and contents, breast milk pumping logs, diaper changes (wet/soiled), baby body temperature, Vitamin D / medication logs, and bath routines.
  4. Infant Solids & Nutrition Data: Solid foods introduced across food groups (vegetables, fruits, proteins, grains, dairy, healthy fats, legumes, seeds, herbs), water intake, exposure to Big 9 food allergens, observed skin or digestive sensitivities/reactions, and saved recipe preferences.
  5. Infant Developmental Milestones Data: Reached developmental milestones and parent notes across Motor skills, Cognitive skills, Speech skills, and Social skills from age 0 to 24 months.

2.3. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Services, we automatically collect limited technical and subscription metadata strictly required to maintain app functionality, billing entitlement verification, security, and local scheduling:

  • Subscription & Entitlement Metadata: Pseudonymous App User UUID, active subscription plan status (`Hazel Pro Annual` or `Hazel Pro Monthly`), trial/renewal expiration dates, auto-renewal flag, and App Store / Google Play purchase tokens validated via RevenueCat. We never collect or store your credit card, debit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay payment details.
  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system type and version, App version, device push notification token (if notifications are enabled), crash logs, and diagnostic performance data.
  • Approximate Region & Time Zone: Your device’s time zone offset and App Store country storefront to accurately calculate daily circadian routines, local 9:00 AM daily card reminders, and regional subscription pricing. We do not collect precise GPS location data.
  • In-App Audio & Local Preferences: Your saved favorite sleep soundscapes, sleep timer durations, and active chapter view settings.

4.4. Family Workspace Synchronization & Member Isolation

If you invite a spouse, partner, grandparent, or caregiver to join your Family Workspace via your 6-character Family Invite Code (up to 8 total members per workspace), logged data within that workspace synchronizes across authorized members' devices. You remain in complete control of your Family Workspace: the Primary Owner may remove any Collaborator at any time, and any Collaborator may leave the workspace at any time. The instant a member leaves or is removed, cryptographic database Row-Level Security (RLS) immediately revokes their read and write access to that Family Workspace.

4.5. Apple In-App Purchase Consumption Data

If you use our App on iOS and make in-app purchases, you consent to our sharing of limited, non-health subscription consumption metadata with Apple strictly to assist Apple in resolving refund requests submitted to Apple, in accordance with Apple’s policies. This never includes any logged health, cycle, pregnancy, or baby care entries.

4.6. Google API Services Limited Use Disclosure

To the extent our Services access, use, or transfer information received from Google APIs, our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: (a) we limit our use of data to providing or improving user-facing features prominent in the App’s interface; (b) we never transfer or sell data to third parties for advertising, data brokerage, or credit-worthiness determination; (c) we do not allow humans to read user data unless we have your affirmative agreement for a specific support request or as required for security/legal compliance; and (d) we never use user data to train generalized or third-party artificial intelligence models.

4.7. Legal Obligations, Vital Safety, and Business Transfers

We may preserve or disclose limited information only if strictly required to do so by applicable law, subpoena, or valid court order, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the vital safety of an individual, prevent fraud or security breaches, or defend against legal claims. In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets of Intervada Solutions LLC, user information may be transferred only if the successor entity commits to bound compliance with the privacy protections set forth in this Privacy Policy and after providing notice to users where required by law.

5. Data Retention and Self-Service Account / Data Deletion

5.1. How Long We Retain Your Data

We retain your personal information and logged data for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide you with the Services. If your subscription pauses or expires without you deleting your account, we preserve your encrypted historical logs in read-safe storage so you do not lose precious family memories if you later renew. If your account remains completely inactive for twenty-four (24) consecutive months, we may delete your inactive account and associated data after sending prior notice to your registered email address. You may trigger immediate deletion at any time.

5.2. How to Permanently Delete Your Account & Data

You have the absolute right to revoke consent and permanently delete your account and personal data at any time through either method below:

  1. In-App Self-Service Deletion (Immediate): Open the Hazel App, navigate to Profile / Settings > Delete Account, and confirm permanent deletion.
  2. Web or Email Deletion Request (If You Uninstalled the App): Submit a request selecting "Account & Data Deletion Request" on our Contact Support page or email support@thehazel.app from the email address registered to your account.

What Happens Upon Deletion & Subscription Billing Reminder:

  • When you trigger in-app deletion, your personal profile, authentication credentials, and owned workspace health logs are immediately purged from our active production database. Email/web deletion requests are processed within fifteen (15) business days (and always within thirty (30) days). Encrypted disaster-recovery backup snapshots roll off automatically within thirty (30) to ninety (90) days.
  • Important Subscription Notice: Deleting your Hazel Account inside the App cannot cancel recurring billing managed by Apple or Google. If you are a Primary Owner with an active Hazel Pro subscription or trial, please also cancel your subscription in your Apple ID Subscriptions or Google Play Subscriptions settings at least 24 hours before your next renewal date.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Global Minors Compliance)

Our Services are directed exclusively to adults (parents, expectant parents, and legal guardians) and are not intended for use by children.

  • Minimum Age Requirements: You must be at least 18 years of age to create an account and use the Services (or at least 16 years of age in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Canada, or 13 years of age in the United States and Australia with verifiable parent or guardian consent).
  • Infant and Child Care Logs: We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Canada). Where you input information about your own baby or toddler into the App—such as your child’s birth date, sleep patterns, feeding history, known food allergens, or reached developmental milestones—that information is provided to us solely by you as the adult parent or legal guardian to assist you in tracking your child’s care.
  • Immediate Removal: If we discover or are notified that a child under the applicable minimum age has registered an account or submitted personal data directly to us without parental consent, we will immediately terminate the account and permanently delete all associated data. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact support@thehazel.app.

7. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical, physical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect sensitive personal and health data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network traffic between the App/Website and our servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3).
  • Encryption at Rest: All cloud database tables, authentication credentials, and backup snapshots are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Cryptographic Row-Level Security (RLS): Our database architecture enforces strict access controls so that your personal and family logs can only be queried by your authenticated user token and active members of your Family Workspace.
  • Strict Internal Access Controls: Access to production infrastructure is restricted to authorized personnel bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Security Incident Notification: While no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% impenetrable, in the unlikely event of a security breach affecting your personal data, we will promptly notify affected users and relevant data protection authorities in accordance with applicable law.

Regardless of where you live, we extend high-standard data protection rights to all users of Hazel:

  1. Right to Access & Know: Confirm whether we process your personal data and receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  2. Right to Data Portability: Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (such as JSON or CSV).
  3. Right to Rectification (Correction): Edit, update, or correct your profile details and logged entries directly within the App at any time, or ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  4. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Delete your account and personal data at any time via the in-app Delete Account button, our Contact Support form, or email.
  5. Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your explicit consent for processing health, fertility, pregnancy, and infant logs at any time.
  6. Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Ask us to restrict or stop processing your personal data in certain circumstances, or disable push notifications anytime in your device settings.

How to Submit a Request & Verification:
Email support@thehazel.app with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request". To protect your sensitive data from unauthorized disclosure, we verify your identity by confirming that the request originates from the email address registered to your account. We respond to all verified requests free of charge within thirty (30) days (or shorter where mandated by local law).
