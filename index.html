From Conception to Toddlerhood

One Calm Companion for Every Chapter of Parenthood

Hazel brings cycle intelligence, week-by-week pregnancy guidance, 24-hour circadian baby sleep dials, 100 first solids, and developmental milestones together in one thoughtful space.

🌸

1. Trying to Conceive (TTC)

Intuitive cycle tracking and ovulation insights designed around your body's natural rhythm.

  • Menstrual cycle & period logging
  • Basal Body Temperature (BBT) & Cervical Mucus
  • Fertile window & ovulation estimates
  • Symptom, hydration, and supplement tracking
🌱

2. Pregnancy Care

Week-by-week fetal growth updates, gentle daily check-ins, and reassuring maternal wellness logs.

  • Week-by-week fetal size & developmental highlights
  • Fetal kick counter & movement tracking
  • Maternal symptoms, appetite, and weight logs
  • Daily hydration, rest, and prenatal routines
🌙

3. Circadian Sleep & Daily Care

Visual 24-hour sleep dials and smart wake windows tailored to your baby's evolving rhythm.

  • Adaptive nap, wake window, and bedtime projections
  • Nursing, bottle feeding, and pumping logs
  • Diaper tracking, bath routines, and soothing reminders
  • Continuous background sleep soundscapes & lullabies
🥑

4. 100 First Solids & Allergens

Guided introduction to solid foods, Big 9 allergen safety ladders, and nutritious food pairings.

  • Interactive 100 First Solids progress dial
  • Big 9 Allergen step-by-step safety tracker
  • Smart iron & brain-boosting food combinations
  • Age-appropriate preparation & texture notes
🧸

5. Milestones & Play

Follow your baby's motor, cognitive, speech, and social growth from birth through 24 months.

  • Stage-by-stage milestones (0 to 24 months)
  • Motor, cognitive, speech, and social development
  • Gentle daily play & activity ideas
  • Grounded in established pediatric guidance
👨‍👩‍👧

6. Partner & Caregiver Sync

Stay effortlessly coordinated with your partner, grandparents, or nanny across every feeding and nap.

  • Real-time shared logs across devices
  • Instant updates for naps, feeds, and diapers
  • Soothing Daylight, Nightfall, and Lavender themes
  • Calm, ad-free experience built for late-night check-ins

✨ Visual 24-Hour Dials

See your baby's sleep windows, 100 first solids, and daily rhythms at a single glance without digging through cluttered spreadsheets.

🩺 Thoughtful & Supportive

Designed to bring calm structure and educational guidance to your daily routine alongside your pediatrician's care.

🤝 Shared Family Rhythm

Whether you're logging a 3 AM feeding or checking off a new solid food, everyone caring for your baby stays on the same page.
